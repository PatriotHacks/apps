## 1. What this covers

This policy covers the PatriotHacks application platform: the site where you create an account, fill
out an application, and receive decisions, and the internal console organizers use to review those
applications.

It does not cover the event itself, anything you hand to a sponsor in person, third-party sites we
link to, or any separate PatriotHacks marketing site.

## 2. What we collect

**Account information.** When you sign in, we store your email address and, if your sign-in method
supplies one, your display name. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never
stored by us in a readable form. If you sign in with Google or GitHub instead, we receive your email
address and name from that provider and nothing else.

**Application answers.** Everything you type into an application form. The questions are set by the
organizers for each event and typically include your name, school, date of birth, your experience
with hackathons, free-text answers about your interests and goals, dietary restrictions, and T-shirt
size. Some of these are more sensitive than others. Dietary restrictions can reveal a religious
practice or a medical condition, and we treat them accordingly: they are used to order food and for
nothing else.

**Draft answers.** Forms save as you go. If you start an application and never submit it, we still
hold what you entered.

**Answer history.** When you change an answer, or when changing a branching answer discards a
section you had already filled in, the previous values are kept in an internal revision history.
This history is not visible to you in the application, is not editable, and is retained for the
lifetime of the record. It exists so organizers can tell what an application said at the time it was
reviewed. See section 8 for how to have it removed.

**Review records.** Organizers attach review notes and a decision (accept, waitlist, reject) to your
application, along with your RSVP status if you are accepted. Review notes are written by organizers
about your application and are not shown to you.

**Email records.** For every email we send you, we keep the recipient address, the subject, the
delivery status, and the identifier our email provider assigned to it. This log is how we prove a
decision notice was actually sent, and it is kept separately from your account.

## 3. What we do not collect

We want to be specific about this, because most privacy policies are vague here.

- No analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no product analytics, and no tracking pixels.
- No advertising, and no sale or sharing of your data with advertisers. Ever.
- No IP address or browser fingerprint logging by us. Our hosting and infrastructure providers keep
  their own operational logs, which we do not control and do not use.
- No tracking of whether you open our emails or click links in them.
- No third-party cookies, and no cookies at all beyond the sign-in session described below.
- No sponsor access. Sponsors do not receive applicant data from this platform. If a future event
  shares anything with sponsors, we will ask you for consent at the point of collection and update
  this policy first.

## 4. Cookies

The only cookies we set are the session cookies our authentication provider uses to keep you signed
in and to refresh your session as you move between pages. They are required for the site to work at
all. Signing out clears them.

## 5. Why we use your information

We use what you give us to run the event and for nothing else:

- To review your application and reach an admission decision.
- To email you about that decision, to confirm your RSVP, and to send logistics you need in order to
  attend.
- To plan the event itself: how much food to order, which dietary restrictions to accommodate, how
  many shirts to print, how many people to expect and when.
- To keep the platform secure and working, and to resolve disputes about what was submitted or sent.

We do not use your application answers to train machine learning models, and we do not use them for
any purpose unrelated to running the event.

## 6. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers. Each one only receives what it needs.

| Provider | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Your account record and your entire application, since it hosts our database and authentication | Storage and sign-in |
| Resend | Your email address, your name, and the content of emails we send you | Email delivery |
| Google or GitHub | Nothing from us. If you choose one to sign in, we receive your email and name from them | Optional sign-in |
| Our hosting provider | Requests to the site, subject to their operational logging | Serving the application |

Beyond these, we share your information only when the law requires it, or when it is necessary to
protect someone's safety.

## 7. Who inside PatriotHacks can see it

Be aware of this before you apply. Every organizer with access to the review console can read every
application in full, including your name, email, all of your answers, your revision history, and the
notes other organizers have written about you. Access is not scoped to applications you were
assigned. Organizer accounts are created manually by an administrator and are limited to the
volunteer team running the event.

Access is enforced in the database rather than in application code, so an organizer account cannot
reach anything the database has not granted it.

## 8. Retention, and your choices

Right now we do not delete application data on a schedule. It stays until you ask us to remove it,
or until the organizing team retires the records for an event.

You can ask us to do any of the following by emailing [privacy@patriothacks.org] from the address on
your account:

- **See what we hold.** We will send you a copy of your account record, your answers, your revision
  history, and your review status.
- **Correct something.** After you submit, you can edit whichever questions the organizers left
  unlocked. For anything locked, ask us and we will change it.
- **Delete your data.** We will delete your account, your application, your answers, and your
  revision history.

These requests are handled manually by a person, not by a button in the app. Allow up to 30 days.

Two limits on deletion, stated plainly. We keep the record that we sent you a particular email,
including the address it went to, even after your account is gone, because it is our proof that a
decision was delivered. And if you attended an event, we may keep a minimal record that you attended
for the organizing team's own history.

## 9. Email and unsubscribing

Announcements and general updates carry an unsubscribe link. Use it and we will stop sending you
that kind of mail.

Transactional email does not respect the unsubscribe list, and this is deliberate. If you have an
open application, we will send you the decision on it and the information you need to attend, even
if you have unsubscribed from announcements. An admission decision is not marketing, and we are not
willing to leave you without one. If you want no email from us at all, ask us to delete your
account.

Your unsubscribe link is unique to you. Treat it like a password and do not share it.

## 10. Age

This platform is for applicants who are 18 or older, as stated in our Terms of Service. We do not
knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an
account, email [privacy@patriothacks.org] and we will delete it.

## 11. Security

Access to applicant data is enforced by database-level policies rather than by application code, so
the boundary holds even if a bug slips through the interface. Data is encrypted in transit.
Administrative access is limited to the organizing team.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your information, we will tell you.

## 12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects how we handle your information, we will
email the address on your account before it takes effect. Otherwise we will update the date at the
top.
